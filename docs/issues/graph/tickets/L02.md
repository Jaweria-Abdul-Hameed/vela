# L02: Tray, background operation, hide-on-close, and safe pause on close

**Planned labels:** `type:feature` `area:lifecycle` `risk:high` `phase:5` `ready-for-agent`  
**Depth in the DAG:** 13 · **Direct blockers (published, transitively reduced):** SP01, R04

> Symbolic key: `L02` · Area: lifecycle · Phase: 5 · Risk: high

## 2. Objective
With background enabled, closing the UI does not stop orchestration and the tray offers reopen, status, and Stop All; otherwise closing offers a safe pause.

## 3. Requirement IDs
FR-042, FR-021, AT-019

## 4. User-visible behavior
Tray menu and the close-confirmation behavior.

## 5. Technical scope
- Hide-on-close with prevent_exit (per spike result); tray menu (reopen, status, Stop All, quit); safe pause path when background operation is off.
- Optional configurable global Stop All shortcut through the global-shortcut plugin (default off, FR-021). Single-instance behavior belongs to L04 only.

## 6. Architectural constraints
- Background operation, auto-start, and consent are opt-in and persisted (ADR-010, ADR-012).
- Closing the window never silently abandons work.

## 7. Dependencies / blocked by
- SP01 Spike S-TRAY: does prevent_exit with a tray keep the Tauri process alive after the last window closes?
- R04 Stop All, pause, and resume for running workers
