## 2. Objective
Before the distributable release, a production Windows Authenticode signing path and a production Tauri updater keypair exist, are held under documented custody, and have their non-secret identifiers recorded; private material never enters the repository. This is a production-release prerequisite, not a development prerequisite: development and testing proceed with development and test signing material (Z01, Z02).

## 5. Technical scope
- Two distinct signatures are in scope and must never be confused. (A) **Tauri updater artifact signing**: required by the Tauri updater, verifies updater artifacts with its own keypair, and is not Windows Authenticode. (B) **Windows Authenticode code signing**: signs the installer, `vela-hook`, and other applicable Windows binaries, and is separate from the updater signature. Every statement in this ticket and its evidence names which signature it means.
- Owns the production decisions, all intentionally unresolved until the release-signing phase and all the user's: the production Authenticode provider or route (a certificate from a certification authority, a managed signing service, or another publicly trusted route), certificate or service purchase and any spend, the identity-validation route, the production signing credentials, production updater private-key generation and custody, and the final updater hosting location. Do not select a vendor on the user's behalf and do not spend money; cost figures are only as classified in `docs/research/H04_signing_research_2026-10-03.md` (verified, unverified or illustrative, or unknown).
- Execute only when the production release-signing procedure is intentionally run: generate the production updater keypair then, keep the private key and any credentials in the CI secret store and a personal password manager only, document custody and backup before release (a lost production updater private key can prevent updating installed copies), and configure the secrets and variables the release workflow reads.
- Record the non-secret identifiers in `docs/release/SIGNING.md` (no secrets): the Authenticode route and publisher identity or thumbprint, the production updater public key and fingerprint, the updater hosting location, and the custody and rotation procedure.
- Development and test signing material (throwaway self-signed certificates, disposable test updater keys) belongs to Z01 and Z02, is never recorded here as trust material, and is never promoted to production.

- [ ] Custody and backup of the production updater private key, and custody of the Authenticode credentials, are documented without secrets before release.
