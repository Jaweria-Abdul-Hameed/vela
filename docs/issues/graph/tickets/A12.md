# A12: Conversation visibility in Antigravity Desktop: read model, Open in Antigravity action, and the AT-026 harness

**Planned labels:** `type:feature` `area:antigravity` `risk:high` `phase:5` `ready-for-agent`  
**Depth in the DAG:** 12 · **Direct blockers (published, transitively reduced):** SP08, U07, S11, A10, A11

> Symbolic key: `A12` · Area: antigravity · Phase: 5 · Risk: high

## 2. Objective
Each in-scope conversation id is recorded and shown with an Open in Antigravity action limited to what the spike verified; the requirement fails honestly where it cannot be satisfied.

## 3. Requirement IDs
FR-050, AT-026

## 4. User-visible behavior
Inspector and timeline show the conversation id and an Open in Antigravity action with clear not-openable states.

## 5. Technical scope
- Conversation ids are persisted by the tickets that own the sessions (workers in S10, reviewer cycles in S11, the substantive analyst in A11); this ticket adds the read model across the three session kinds, the inspector's Open in Antigravity action limited to the SP08-verified mechanism, and the AT-026 harness.
- Where the mechanism is unverified or a conversation cannot be opened, the inspector says so and the AT-026 harness fails the requirement rather than passing on Vela's own rendering.

## 6. Architectural constraints
- Only vela-adapters::antigravity and vela-uia may contain Antigravity-specific behavior (ADR-008, ADR-018).
- Never trust exit code or result.status; interpret events, stderr, and Git state.
- Never read, copy, or persist Antigravity credentials or tokens (ADR-016).
- Elements tagged [U] are gated by their spike; do not claim them ready.

## 7. Dependencies / blocked by
- SP08 Spike S-DESKTOP-VISIBILITY: are headless CLI conversations visible and openable in Antigravity Desktop?
- U07 Issue focus transition and inspector with progressive disclosure
- S11 Checkpoint and review loop with a fake reviewer: fixed point, findings, fix loop, cap
- A10 Authoritative reviewer session: fresh conversation, code-review at the fixed point, structured output
- A11 Dependency analyst session: read-only, validated, stored in the snapshot
