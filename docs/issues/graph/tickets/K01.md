# K01: Approval Broker core: classification pipeline, evidence tiers, decisions, rules, interventions

**Planned labels:** `type:feature` `area:broker` `risk:high` `phase:2` `ready-for-agent`  
**Depth in the DAG:** 7 · **Direct blockers (published, transitively reduced):** D07, S15

> Symbolic key: `K01` · Area: broker · Phase: 2 · Risk: high

## 2. Objective
Approval requests are classified by policy with evidence tiers; ALLOW is delivered, ASK and DENY become interventions or refusals, and everything is journaled.

## 3. Requirement IDs
FR-031, FR-035, FR-039, AT-012

## 4. User-visible behavior
None (no user-visible behavior).

## 5. Technical scope
- Broker actor; evidence-binding rules; decision persistence (requests, decisions, delivery attempts, policy rules); journal events; DENY outcomes (POLICY_DENIED, APPROVAL_UNDELIVERABLE); unsafe request never auto-approved (AT-012).
- Registers the approval reconciliation recipe with R01 (never replay a stale decision or click) and declares FaultPoints `approval.decision_persisted` and `approval.delivery_attempted` (SHARED_SURFACE_PROTOCOL.md section 6).

## 6. Architectural constraints
- Policy and delivery are separate; no adapter decides safety (ADR-007).
- Vela approves operations, not buttons; fail closed on any ambiguity.
- UI automation is opt-in and consent-gated (ADR-010).

## 7. Dependencies / blocked by
